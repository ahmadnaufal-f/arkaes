**SmartThings Find** brings Samsung's location finding experience to iPhone users through the SmartThings app. iOS users can open it from the *Life* tab to locate supported devices and items.

Samsung has announced **Galaxy SmartTag3** as the first Galaxy SmartTag with cross platform support for Galaxy and iOS devices. With SmartTag3, supported iOS users will be able to check and share a tag's location and use *Search Nearby* through SmartThings Find.

My main responsibility was **leading the development team** at **Samsung R&D Institute Indonesia**. I guided the team through a broad set of requirements within a tight development schedule, coordinating technical decisions, reviewing implementations, resolving engineering challenges, and keeping the team aligned toward delivery.

Despite the scope and a relatively small team, we completed our responsibilities **on schedule** while maintaining the quality required for a production release. Alongside my leadership responsibilities, I contributed directly to the frontend architecture and helped shape the **map architecture** so map specific behavior remained separate from application logic as the product evolved.

This project is meaningful to me because it supports a more **cross platform SmartThings ecosystem**. With Galaxy SmartTag3, supported iOS users will be able to use SmartThings Find alongside Galaxy users, making the experience more useful for families and groups using a mix of devices. Seeing SmartThings Find become available on iOS and SmartTag3's compatibility announcement is especially rewarding after leading an amazing team that helped bring the experience to production.
